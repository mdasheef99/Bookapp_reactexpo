## Part A: message to send

**About us.** BookConnect is a brand of our existing LLP. It is a marketplace app where
independent Bengaluru bookstores sell new and used books.
- **Who sells:** each store is the seller; BookConnect is the marketplace.
- **Checkout:** customers pay first through Cashfree. The book is reserved for 10 minutes
  while they pay, and each store's share is settled to the store by Cashfree.
- **Fulfilment:** customers either pick up at the store with a one-time code, or get
  same-day bike delivery that BookConnect books with a delivery provider.
- **Pilot:** five stores; for three months stores pay no commission or subscription and
  customers pay no platform fee. Stores pay the Cashfree payment fee and customers pay the
  delivery charge.

### Marketplace structure and store agreement

1. What contracts make the store the seller and BookConnect only the marketplace?
2. Please check these store-agreement clauses for enforceability:
   - the Cashfree payment fee is deducted from the store's share;
   - when a refund is the store's fault, the store bears the Cashfree fee;
   - a refund paid after the store was paid is recovered from its next payout, and the
     store repays within 7 days otherwise;
   - starter limits: 20 paid orders a day and ₹25,000 unsettled;
   - suspension after 3 store faults in 30 days, or at once for suspected fraud or an
     unpaid debt;
   - future pricing changes need 30 days' notice and in-app acceptance;
   - liability limits;
   - who handles and pays for refunds, disputes and chargebacks;
   - when a delivery fails, a return trip is needed, or the delivery bill is above the quote
     because of the store, the store bears the book and the delivery costs (BookConnect
     absorbs the first ₹50 of a bill above the quote);
   - the store must mark counter sales sold promptly and answer return or dispute cases
     within 24 of its business hours;
   - store payouts are made 3 days after handover, in the next Monday payout.
3. Can BookConnect hold back a store's money during disputes, suspected fraud or
   chargebacks? Can we still recover a store's debt if the store stops selling? Does holding a
   store's share until after handover, paying refunds in advance, or collecting the delivery
   charge risk treating BookConnect as holding customer money under the RBI payment
   aggregator rules?

### Customer terms (Consumer Protection Act, 2019)

4. Are these customer terms enforceable?
   - Pay-first checkout with a 10-minute reservation.
   - If the store does not accept a paid order within 24 hours, it is cancelled with an
     automatic full refund.
   - When a book in an order is unavailable, the store phones the customer and records in
     the app whether the customer keeps the rest (the missing books are refunded) or cancels
     everything (full refund). If the customer cannot be reached within 24 hours, the rest
     is kept and the missing books are refunded.
   - When a delivery fails, or is cancelled after collection, because of the customer, we
     deduct delivery costs. The refund is the amount paid minus the quoted delivery charge
     minus the actual return-trip cost, never below zero. Is this allowed under the 2020
     E-Commerce Rules' limits on cancellation charges? If a customer misses a return pickup
     twice, the second failed trip's cost is deducted and the customer must bring the book
     to the store.
   - A pickup customer may cancel for a full refund until the store marks the order ready.
   - If no rider accepts a delivery, it is rebooked for the next day; the customer may
     cancel for a full refund.
   - A returned book is refunded only after the store inspects it.
   - No change-of-mind returns for new or used books, only for defects, damage, a wrong
     edition, missing pages or misdescription.
   - A 48-hour target for reporting condition problems. This target is not a cutoff on
     legal rights.
   - A 3-day pickup window, counting only days the store is open; a missed pickup is
     cancelled and fully refunded.
   - Same-day delivery is shown as an estimate ("expected today"), with no compensation if
     late.
5. Privacy (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023): we share customers' phone numbers
   and addresses with stores, the delivery provider and Cashfree. What must the privacy
   policy say? Must it name Cashfree and the delivery provider as processors? We also hold
   stores' KYC and bank details: what must we tell stores about that data? Our ledger
   cannot be edited, and we keep evidence photos and finance records: how do these sit with
   a deletion request, and how long must or may we keep them?
6. Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020:
   - Which seller details must we display?
   - May the owner be the grievance officer?
   - What is the minimum complaint-ticket system (48-hour acknowledgement, resolution
     within one month)?
   - Which policy pages do we need (terms, privacy, refund and cancellation, pickup,
     contact)?
   - Must we obtain a written undertaking from each store that its listings are accurate,
     and which exact clauses of Rules 5 and 6 apply to us and to the stores?
   - We read that E-Commerce Amendment Rules, 2026 take effect on 1 January 2027. Which of
     their duties apply to us?

### Delivery

7. BookConnect will contract with the bike-delivery provider and book the rider. What is
   BookConnect's liability to customers for lost or damaged parcels? What should we check
   in the provider's terms (liability caps, claims, charges)?

### Refund routes

8. Refunds normally go back to the original payment method unless the customer asks
   otherwise. If a refund to the original account fails (for example a closed account), which
   other refund route is permitted?
9. If a customer is still entitled to a refund after Cashfree's refund time limit (between
   120 days and six months, depending on the document), how may we refund them lawfully?

### Pickup, photos, returns and store refunds

10. **Pickup by code.** Anyone who shows the customer's valid one-time code may collect the
    order, and store staff may verify the code. A customer may refuse a damaged, wrong or
    missing book at pickup. Does the customer agreement need wording on collection by someone
    else, and on who is liable if the wrong person collects?
11. **Photo check before handover.** If a customer pays while their request for extra photos
    is still open, the store must send the photos within the same 24 hours it has to accept
    the order, or the order is refunded automatically. The order is not handed over until the
    customer approves. If the customer rejects the photos, or does not reply within 24 hours,
    the order is cancelled with a full refund. Is that fine, and what must the terms say?
12. **Returns and disputes.** Every return and dispute needs BookConnect admin approval. An
    approved return is refunded after the store's inspection passes; a failed inspection
    becomes a dispute. If the customer does not hand the book over within 5 days of
    approval, the return closes without a refund. Is that consistent with consumer rights?
    What must the customer terms say?
13. **Store-side clauses.** Should the store agreement contain these?
    - A ban on settling price differences or payments with customers outside BookConnect.
    - A rule that a store may ask for a refund only through BookConnect, never refunding
      customers directly.
